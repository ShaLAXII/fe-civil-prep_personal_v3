## Confirmed source issues

- **Learnova 3 (PDF p.10):** Exact area of minor circular segment for radius 10 m and chord 16 m is about **44.7295 m²**, not the source's **44.43 m²** (B). B is the nearest listed choice; question is not suitable for exact grading.
- **Learnova 68 (PDF p.30):** With values 40, 42, 44, 41, 43, *population* standard deviation is **1.414** (A); *sample* standard deviation is **1.581** (D). Question does not specify which interpretation is intended. Not graded.
- **Learnova 620 (PDF p.228):** Extracted solution explains floodplain zoning rather than the option about green roofs/permeable pavement; needs source-specific explanation review. Not graded.
- **Learnova 18, 276, 277:** Existing manually noted source-key corrections are retained from the previous version.

## Important limitations

- There are **802 chapter-numbered Islam records** because the source contents sum that way despite the cover describing 800 problems; these remain mostly OCR-derived. Their figures and formulas cannot be trusted without manual source-page reading.
- Some original book diagrams and tables are **not reproduced** in the text website. A question can be browseable but unusable for accurate answer selection.
- The 791 nonflagged source transcriptions may still contain undetected errors in math, numerical options, publisher solutions, or context. Scores from these are provisional.
- The 110-question mock matches the NCEES count and duration but is not an official simulation and is not calibrated to exam difficulty.
- Reconstructed Learnova main-book items and Islam indexed problems are all included; four separate Learnova practice exams are not included.
- See **QUESTION_REVIEW_STATUS.csv** for one record per question and **REVIEW_CORRECTIONS.csv** for changes and flags.

## Recommended study use

Use *Checked practice* to minimize transcription risk. For graded source-key practice, compare any doubtful answer with the original textbook and FE Handbook. Do not treat a publisher-key match as proof of technical correctness.
